Home · Services

Every step, handled with the seriousness it deserves

Our services go beyond admissions processing. We work with students and young professionals to build the knowledge, confidence, and credentials they need to compete globally.

01 · Educational Consultancy

Choosing right, from the start

We start by mapping your grades, budget, and goals against what schools actually require, not a generic country list. That means asking hard questions early, can you afford it, will you get the visa, does the programme actually lead where you want to go, so you're not discovering a mismatch after you've already paid application fees.

02 · International Admission

Placement, done properly

We place students directly with partner universities in Germany, Italy, Georgia, India, and Mauritius, among other destinations, plus platform access to more. We know each school's actual requirements and deadlines, and we handle the paperwork end to end so the student can focus on being ready.

03 · Scholarship & Financial Aid

Funding shouldn't be the wall

We match students against scholarships they're realistically eligible for, not a generic list scraped off the internet, then manage the application itself: forms, essays, and deadlines. Funding should never be the reason a qualified student stays behind.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I start the process with Cipher?

Book a consultation through the form on our homepage, or reach out by phone, email, or WhatsApp. We start by understanding your background, goals, and budget before recommending a path forward.

Do I need to already know which school or country I want?

No. Many students come to us with no fixed idea. Part of our job is helping you narrow down the right country, school, and programme based on your goals, grades, and budget.

Which countries do you place students in?

We work across destinations including Turkiye, Italy, Germany, Georgia, Hungary, the UK, Canada, China, Mauritius, and India, with direct partnerships at several universities and platform access to more.

Can you help me find a scholarship?

Yes. We help students identify scholarships they genuinely qualify for and guide the application itself. Not every student ends up fully funded, and we'll be honest with you about what's realistic for your situation rather than promising guarantees.

Do you help with visa applications too?

Yes, visa documentation and process support is part of what we do once admission is secured, so you're not navigating that alone after the hard part of getting in is already done.

How much does it cost to work with Cipher?

It depends on the service and destination. Book a consultation and we'll walk you through the actual costs for your specific situation rather than quoting a generic number that may not apply to you.

Can a parent book the consultation instead of the student?

Yes, parents book consultations on behalf of their children regularly, particularly for younger students. We're happy to work with whoever is guiding the decision.

Not sure which service you need?

Book a session and we'll map out the right path together.
